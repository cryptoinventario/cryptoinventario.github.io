Widgets

Small standalone pages made to fit the Crypto Inventario wall, and just as happy on their own. Open one, or use its link anywhere you can embed a web page.

In Crypto Inventario, add a block and type [clock], [calculator], [calendar] or [eye]. A block is the same as embedding https://host/widgets/clock.html. Widgets use your device's own time and need no account or network.
